Workflow

Five Steps from
Idea to Live

Transparent at every stage — you always know where your project stands.

01

Consultation

Free 30-minute discussion to map goals, target users, and visual references. Output: needs summary and package recommendation.

02

Proposal & Agreement

We send proposal, scope of work, and written timeline. Once agreed, the down payment marks project kickoff.

03

Design

Wireframe then UI design. You review and provide feedback until the design is approved before a single line of code is written.

04

Development

Coding, integration, and testing. You get a preview link and weekly progress reports.

05

Launch & Support

Deploy to your domain, usage training, source code handover, then warranty period begins.
