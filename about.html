The journey

From Jember,
To The Country.

No shortcuts, no inherited playbook — just nine years of shipping work, learning in public, and building a team that outgrew every room we started in.

The foundation

Pasuruan → Jember

SMPN 1 Pasuruan, then SMAN 1 Pasuruan, then a Bachelor of Information Systems at Universitas Jember. That's where the two halves came together: systems thinking on one side, visual storytelling on the other.

2017

Nano Indonesia is born

Started as a small digital yearbook project for local schools. No investor, no safety net — just a laptop, a camera, and a conviction that graduation memories deserved better than a stack of printed pages.

Building the craft

From yearbooks to full production

The service line expanded: creative production house, AR technology, and full event organizing. Each new division came from a real client problem, not a business plan slide.

Scaling up

A team of 40, a country of clients

What began solo became a 40-person creative house handling 50+ school deals per cycle. Systems, CRM, internal PWAs — I built the tools we needed because nothing off the shelf fit how we work.

Today

231+ clients and still building

Digital yearbooks, company websites, web apps, AR experiences, and graduation productions — with a 4.9 average rating across 50+ school collaborations. Still hands-on, still shipping.

How I work

Three Rules
I Never Break

Strategy before pixels

Beautiful work that misses the business goal is decoration. Every project starts with what the client is actually trying to win — then the design follows.

Ship it, then refine it

A live product teaches you more in a week than a plan does in a month. I set clear timelines up front and get real work in front of real users fast.

Founder-level ownership

I've built a company from zero, so I treat every engagement like equity. No hand-offs, no disappearing after launch — I stay accountable for the outcome.
